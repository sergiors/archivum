# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Archivum is used by one person to browse their own backed-up email and WhatsApp history. They use it when they need to find, read, or orient themselves in past communication without taking part in the conversation.

## Product Purpose

Archivum organizes archived emails and WhatsApp conversations into source workspaces. A selected mailbox or WhatsApp source scopes the navigation, search, and reader so the archive feels understandable rather than like one undifferentiated stream.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, trustworthy, organized. The interface should feel like a carefully indexed personal record: calm to browse, easy to scan, and focused on retrieval rather than communication.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, inbox action bars, reply/compose controls, analytics cards, decorative marketing surfaces, and any visual language that implies live messaging or active collaboration.

## Design Principles

- Make source context visible before content.
- Optimize for reading and retrieval, not action.
- Keep archive density high but give long-form content room to breathe.
- Use selection and hierarchy to make large histories feel navigable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced motion. Color should reinforce source context without being the only way to distinguish content.
